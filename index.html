Preparation Steps

  1. Wash the vegetables

    Wash the bell peppers, mushrooms, squashes, asparagus, chayote squash, and carrots well.

  2. Cut into slices

    Slice the vegetables into even pieces so they cook nicely in the pan.

  3. Heat avocado oil in the skillet

    Add avocado oil to a skillet and warm it up over medium heat.

  4. Cook and stir with a spatula

    Add the sliced vegetables to the pan and move them around with a spatula as they cook.

  5. Season to taste and serve

    Add seasonings to taste, stir briefly, and serve warm alongside your Thanksgiving meal.

Quick Tip & Roasted Veggie History

Roasting vegetables is a technique far older than Thanksgiving, with roots stretching back to prehistoric times.

  • Roasting vegetables is a technique far older than Thanksgiving, with roots stretching back to prehistoric times.
  • In rural Catalonia, the traditional escalivada dish was born from cooking eggplants, peppers, and onions directly over dying embers.
  • Before modern ovens, roasting over hot coals or stones was one of the most common ways to make vegetables tastier and easier to digest
