Golden Roasted Vegetables

A warm, easy side dish for your Thanksgiving table. Sweet, crispy, and full of autumn color.

Preparation Steps

  1. Heat the oven

    Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C). Place a large baking sheet inside while the oven heats up.

  2. Wash and cut

    Wash and peel the vegetables. Cut them into pieces of the same size, about 1 inch (2.5 cm), so they cook evenly.

  3. Season

    Put all the vegetables in a large bowl. Add the olive oil, garlic, salt, pepper, and herbs. Mix well with your hands until every piece is covered.

  4. Spread on the pan

    Carefully spread the vegetables on the hot baking sheet in one layer. Leave some space between the pieces so they roast instead of steam.

  5. Roast

    Roast for 35 to 40 minutes. Stir the vegetables once halfway through. They are ready when the edges are golden brown and a fork goes in easily.

  6. Finish and serve

    Drizzle with maple syrup if you like a sweeter taste. Move to a warm serving dish and serve right away.

Quick Tip & Turkey Pairing

Roasted vegetables are a perfect partner for roast turkey. Their sweet, crispy flavor balances the soft, savory meat.

  • Roast the vegetables while the turkey rests. The oven is free, and the turkey stays juicy.
  • Spoon a little turkey gravy over the vegetables for extra flavor.
  • Add dried cranberries or chopped pecans on top for a festive finish.
